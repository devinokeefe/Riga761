## 3. Material from third parties: not covered by the licences above

- `images/telegram761.jpg` is page 21 of the scan of file I.301.8.663, part 1, as published online by the
  Centralne Archiwum Wojskowe (Wojskowe Biuro Historyczne), Warsaw, which holds the original. It is reproduced
  here with the archive's watermark, reduced in size and with the scanner's border trimmed. Ask the archive
  about reusing it.
- `data/control_texts.txt` has the opening passages of six Soviet diplomatic documents of October 1920, as
  printed in *Документы внешней политики СССР*, vol. 3 (Moscow, 1959). They are official documents and no
  rights are claimed in them here.
- `data/russian_trigrams.tsv` is a table of letter statistics counted from the same volume. The volume's text is
  not included.
